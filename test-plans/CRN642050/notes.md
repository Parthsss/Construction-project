# CRN642050 — Ms. Sapna

First floor plan. Built-up area is stated on the sheet's area statement (~744 SF).

## What makes this one interesting

**The plot is much bigger than the building.** The site runs about 40' × 70'-3", but the
built area occupies only part of it — the rest is open ground. The plot edge is drawn as
a dash-dot line right on the same sheet.

So "the outermost closed shape on the page" is the wrong answer here. Your boundary is
the built floor, not the land. This is the plan that separates a boundary detector from
a bounding-box.
