# CRN574114 — Mrs. Parvathi

First floor plan. Built-up area is stated on the sheet's area statement (~649 SF).

## What makes this one interesting

**The plot is not square.** The site boundary corners are 91°, 88°, 87° and 93° — not
90°. The building inside it is drawn square-ish, but the outer dash-dot line is skewed,
and the two long sides are not the same length (40'-1" on one side, 39'-6" on the other).

If your code assumes a rectangle, or takes the bounding box of everything on the page,
this plan will show you why that fails.
