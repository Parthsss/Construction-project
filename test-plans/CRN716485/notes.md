## What makes this one interesting

**The architect has already annotated structure onto the plan.** You will see
`CONCEALED BEAM` runs in dashed blue, `FC(8"X18") Starts from first floor roof`,
`Column stops in ground floor roof`, `CHAJJA PROJECTION` arrows and a `LOFT 25sqft`
patch. That is real — architects mark what they have coordinated with the engineer.

Two things follow:

- You may use those hints. Say so in your README if you do.
- "Column stops in ground floor roof" means **not every column runs the full height**.
  If your logic assumes columns always stack, this plan is where that assumption shows.

The balcony (25' × 4'-3") and the chajja projections force the same boundary decision as
the other plans: is a projection part of the floor or not?
